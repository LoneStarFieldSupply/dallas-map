Recommendation

Pick: Knox-Henderson. It is 336 m from the Katy Trail (tied for the trail lead) and 454 m from public tennis at Abbott Park, the closest tennis to any core. The average 2BR is about $2,474 (RentCafe), but real listings at or under $1,900 exist here: 4321 Cole Ave at $1,700–$1,900 and Apartments at M-Streets at $1,595–$1,650 (see the green $ layer). The weak spots are the nearest local gym, about 1.3 km away, and Tenison's $5 range, about 6 km away.

Runner-up: Oak Lawn. It leads for bars (7 within 400 m) and has 9 restaurants. Tennis at Craddock Park is about 1 km away and the Katy Trail about 950 m. The average 2BR is $3,044, but Maverick Oak Lawn lists 2BRs from about $1,505–$1,870.

Value/house option: M Streets / Lakewood. It is near White Rock Lake and Tenison, with duplexes around $1,800. It is much less walkable: OSM shows 0 restaurants within 400 m of the core used here.

On a $1,900 budget: look at older 1950s–70s boutique and garden buildings, duplexes in Junius Heights and Lakewood, Cityplace near the DART station, and properties advertising weeks or months free.

Counts are OpenStreetMap features within 400 m of each core point. OSM undercounts, so treat them as relative. No neighborhood has a driving range within a 5-minute walk.
